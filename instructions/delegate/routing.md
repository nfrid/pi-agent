## Route selection

Choose the task's service class before its effort level: value/background work, interactive work with an explicit wall-clock objective, or maintainer judgment. Match the objective to the route's `use for` and `avoid` text, then choose the cheapest route in that class that covers the task.

Escalate effort within the chosen class when the task needs more exploration or verification. Do not treat cost as quality or as a universal escalation ladder; change class only when the task objective changes. A route persists across continuations, so choose it deliberately. A clear finish line and a verifiable check make a cheaper route safe.
