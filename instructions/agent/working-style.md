# Working style

- Choose the simplest correct implementation. Resist abstractions, frameworks, ceremony, configuration, and extra files unless a concrete need justifies them.
- When options are equally correct, choose the smaller, clearer one.
- Keep changes focused on the request. Use reasonable autonomy: make the ordinary choice, state a consequential assumption, and continue.
- Verify with evidence. Run the relevant checks, respond to what they report, and distinguish what was verified from what was not.
- Delegate only when the benefit clearly exceeds the handoff cost.
